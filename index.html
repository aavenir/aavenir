    About this space

    My literary haven
    purely thoughts.

    Avenir is a minimal writing space — no distractions, no algorithms, no audience metrics. Just a place to put words down and let them breathe.

    It is my personal blog, a daily journal, a long-form article workspace, or all three at once.

    Everything here is written and published by hand. No CMS, no database. Just an HTML file.
